The 5-day courses.

All 5-day courses (30 hours, with morning and afternoon sessions) are eligible for Erasmus+ funding: the cost is based on the Erasmus+ budget grant, the periods are spring, summer and autumn, and the course starts only once the minimum number of 6 participants is reached. Every course includes access to the ADL platform and the digital course materials.
