Erasmus+ KA2 · Strategic Partnerships for Adult Education

OLFA — life skills and financial literacy for adults outside the labour force.

A two-year strategic partnership between five countries of the European Union to build training tools for adults who, for external reasons, have remained outside the workforce. Project 2019-1-DK01-KA204-060229.

The project

A niche that nobody was covering.

Lifelong learning is an important and useful activity at every stage of life. That is especially true when a person's life has changed or stalled in its circumstances, and they need further skills in order to develop their own capabilities.

The project addresses indirectly those adults who have been forced to leave the labour market because of external factors: people who need to change their lives, acquire new knowledge, find new jobs and look for new opportunities. The direct target group, on the other hand, consists of educators, trainers, coaches and adult-learning professionals who work — or want to work — with adults in difficulty, know their problems and are open to welcoming and applying new solutions.

The two-year collaboration involved five countries of the European Union, with adult-education organisations that have extensive experience both in developing the target groups and in using methods already employed to address the project's underlying problem.

2019 — 2022
The context

The «invisible adults».

Some economic inactivity is desirable: people invest in education when they are young, or look for leisure time when they are older. But most of the economic inactivity in Europe cannot be put down to retirement or education.

Many adults of working age leave the labour force because of injury, health problems, disability or premature «retirement»; some people never enter the world of work at all during their lives. The result is a negative economic and social impact, costly social support and wider consequences for European society.

International statistics classify the working-age population as employed, unemployed and outside the labour force, with a sub-classification that identifies the potential labour force: an important measure of labour underutilisation, relevant in recessions or where there are few channels for looking for work. It is to these people that the project is addressed.

Objectives

What the project set out to achieve.

One fundamental objective: to create an intellectual output that combines international experience useful for adult training in all EU countries, promoting individual mobility and the free movement of skilled labour.

01

Core skills for new challenges

To foster the acquisition of life skills that are fundamental for facing new contexts and new working environments, with a specific focus on the financial literacy tied to the success of a person's life path.

02

Innovative solutions

The intellectual outputs seek innovative solutions to the problem of adults left outside the workforce, starting from the methods the partners have already tested with their own target groups.

03

A tool for trainers

To give educators and trainers a practical, use-oriented handbook, with adult-training methods that respond to new challenges and exercises for concrete application.

04

Content from daily life to the future

The professional material covers the widest possible content: from household management and social and welfare knowledge, through saving, to sustainable lifestyles and planning for the future.

05

Sustainability of the results

To guarantee the usefulness of the methodology built, through practical materials that do not merely introduce the new method but make it easier to use and adapt over time.

06

Peer training

Mobility in all the participating countries, with trainers training one another, workshops on the content of the chapters and coordination of the results between one meeting and the next.

Results

The two intellectual outputs.

The first output builds the methodology; the second guarantees its usability and effectiveness in practice. Together they form a complete training system for anyone working with adults outside the labour market.

Output 1

The online methodological collection

A methodological collection built on an online platform, intended mainly for the experts who work with the indirect target group — adults outside the labour force. The aim is to create a new handbook, practice-oriented and easy to use, that includes adult-training methods able to respond to new challenges, together with exercises for practical implementation.

The modules are not built on one another and can be taken separately. Each module closes with a certificate of participation: once the details are filled in, the personalised certificate arrives at the email address given.

Output 2

Exercise workbook and collection of practical tasks

The second intellectual output is responsible for guaranteeing the usefulness of the methodological collection. It consists of exercise workbooks and a collection of practical tasks that, on the operational level, support the usability and efficiency of the methodology built.

During the planned multiplication events, every partner country tested the first output locally: the materials from those tests became the content of the second output. The aim is to support, seriously, the sustainability of the main result of two years of international cooperation.

Mobility

The transnational meetings.

Five stops in five countries: each meeting moved one chapter of the methodology forward, with workshops, exchange between trainers and coordination of the results from one mobility to the next.

Denmark

Køge — 19/01/2020 – 21/01/2020

Transnational meeting

The first meeting was held at the premises of Køge Handelsskole, the lead partner. Three days of work to set the project framework, share the methods already in use at the partner organisations and define the structure of the methodological collection.

Hungary

Eger — 23/08/2021 – 25/08/2021

Joint training C1

Between 23 and 25 August 2021 the C1 training of the OLFA project took place in Eger. All the partners took part except the Spanish partner. The main aims of the training were: to create the new project timeline, to decide the new dates of the forthcoming meetings, to create the framework of the intellectual productions and to divide the project work among the partners.

Belgium

Turnhout — 25/11/2021 – 26/11/2021

Transnational meeting

Two days at the EduKempen premises in Turnhout, to pick the project thread back up after the pause imposed by the pandemic and to check on the progress of the methodological collection.

Spain

Tenerife — 04/04/2022 – 05/04/2022

Transnational meeting

In Tenerife the partnership worked on fine-tuning the modules and on preparing the local multiplication events, with a session devoted to the S.M.A.R.T. methodology applied to setting the participants' objectives.

Italy

Treviso — 30/09/2022 – 01/10/2022

Final meeting

The closing meeting was held in Treviso, hosted by Nefeli Education. Two days devoted to the final check of the outputs, to disseminating the results and to closing the project, with a visit to Venice.
