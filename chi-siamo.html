The institution

An educational nucleus with an international vocation.

Nefeli Education was founded as an Educational Nucleus for European training for international work: an organisation that brings together language training, career guidance and methodological research, with a network of academic collaborations in Europe, Asia and North America.

Our mission

Removing three obstacles: cost, curriculum, culture.

We believe that access to quality education should not depend on socioeconomic background, culture of origin or field of study. We work on these three fronts with concrete tools: pathways funded by European programmes, study programmes built on real needs, and teaching that recognises difference as a resource.

We are experts in and ambassadors of international exchange as a discovery of oneself and of others. Through partnerships with organisations that share this vision, we help students, teachers and young professionals understand the value of intercultural exchange.

Guiding principles

The convictions that guide our work.

01

Community first

All people deserve to live in thriving, sustainable communities. Strong communities depend on well-educated, innovative and ethical leaders, and on informed and engaged citizens.

02

Research and rigour

Creative, current and rigorous research informs good decision-making. We do not teach what we cannot document.

03

Skills to decide

Leaders need both relevant information and the skills to interpret and use it: data, not just opinions.

04

Beyond sector boundaries

Successful leaders know how to cross the boundaries between public, private and non-profit. Conversation between different perspectives produces better solutions.

05

Access, not selection

We work so that all students, regardless of background, have access to a quality international learning experience.

06

Measure to improve

Every pathway has outcome indicators. If a module does not work, we change it: assessment is part of the method, not a formality.

Accreditations

Exam centres and recognitions.

We are recognised as an exam centre by international certification bodies: this means that preparation and certification take place in the same place, to the same standards.

United Kingdom

Trinity College London

Exam centre for the GESE (spoken exam) and ISE (integrated skills) English language certifications. Recognised in the world of schools and training, level by level.

United States — ETS

TOEFL iBT and TOEIC

Exam centre for TOEFL iBT, the academic English test for admission to Italian and foreign universities, and for TOEIC, the standard for professional English.

Italy

CECOL L2 — University of Reggio Calabria

Accredited as an exam centre for the CECOL Italian language certification: certification of Italian as a second language, useful for residence permits, university and work.

European Union

Erasmus+ and Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs

Partner in European cooperation projects (Erasmus+ KA2, OLFA project — 2019-1-DK01-KA204-060229) and accredited host organisation for entrepreneurs in the Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs programme. Discover the OLFA project.
